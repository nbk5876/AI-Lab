Qwen2.5-Coder:14B

The first model installed on the lab's first node. A coding-oriented model — strong at writing and reasoning about code, but it noticeably leans toward structured, tool-call-style output even for casual questions in Open WebUI (see Engineering Notebook, Note 005).

Lesson learned

Coder models aren't automatically good everyday chatbots. Different jobs call for differently-tuned models.

Next up: a conversational model

To get a more natural chat experience in Open WebUI, the plan is to pull down qwen3:8b or qwen3:14b — both general-purpose conversational models, sized to run comfortably on the Beelink's 32 GB of RAM.

Choosing a model size

Rough guidance for hardware like the Beelink SER9 (32 GB RAM, no discrete GPU):

  • 7B–9B — comfortable (e.g. Llama 3.1 8B, Mistral 7B, Gemma 2 9B)
  • 13B–14B — feasible with 4-bit quantization, but slower
  • 30B+ — will load, but crawls without a GPU to offload to
