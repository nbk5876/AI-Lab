Tony and Max are building a small AI lab out of consumer hardware sitting on their desks — no data center, no cloud bill, just a couple of mini PCs learning to think locally. This site is the public record of that build: what we install, what breaks, what we learn, and what we'd tell you if you wanted to build one too.

The stack so far leans on Ollama for running models locally, Open WebUI as the chat front end, Claude Code as our engineering assistant for building and documenting the lab itself, and Tailscale to mesh our machines together over a private network. Everything here — hardware notes, model comparisons, mistakes, and lessons — is written up as we go and shared openly.

Our motto

Build. Document. Share. Improve.
