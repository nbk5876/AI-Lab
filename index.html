We're building a small AI lab out of consumer hardware sitting on our desks — no data center, no cloud bill, just a couple of mini PCs learning to think locally. This site is the public record of that build: what we install, what breaks, what we learn, and what we'd tell you if you wanted to build one too.

The stack so far leans on Ollama for running models locally, Open WebUI as the chat front end, Claude Code as our engineering assistant for building and documenting the lab itself, and Tailscale to mesh our machines together over a private network. Everything here — hardware notes, model comparisons, mistakes, and lessons — is written up as we go and shared openly.

Launch the AI Lab

Already on the shared Tailscale tailnet or the home LAN? Open the chat app directly:

http://ai-lab-b:8080

Not connected to the tailnet? This link won't load — see Field Guide Part 2 for how mobile/remote access is set up.
