The AI Lab isn't about proving anyone's an AI expert — it's about documenting the process of learning by building. You don't need any special background to follow along, and there's no wrong way to start.

Our motto

Build. Document. Share. Improve.

Ways to get involved

Observer

Observer

Follow the Build Journal and Field Guide. No setup required — just read along as the lab grows.

Builder

Builder

Set up your own local AI node — Ollama, Open WebUI, and a model or two — using the lab's notes as a starting point.

Contributor

Contributor

Share your own discoveries, model comparisons, or fixes back with the lab — corrections and new notes are always welcome.

Collaborator

Collaborator

Join in on shared experiments — connect your own node, help extend the architecture, or take on a piece of the roadmap.

Whichever level fits, the goal is the same: learn how modern local AI actually works, one honest build log at a time.
